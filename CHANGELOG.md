# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Reopens the most recently closed tab of the window you are in, and nothing else. Shortcut: Option+Shift+T on a Mac,
  Alt+Shift+T on Windows and Linux.
- Also asks for Firefox's own keys (Cmd+Shift+T on a Mac, Ctrl+Shift+T on Windows and Linux). They become active once
  Firefox's own shortcut is removed in `about:keyboard`.
- Optional settings page that works out the numbers for keeping closed tabs and windows for longer.
- One permission: `sessions`. Requires Firefox 140 or newer.
